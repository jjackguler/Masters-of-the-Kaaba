## Full description (EN)
605 CE. One winter night a great flood swept through Mecca and cracked the walls of the Kaaba. Quraysh decided to take it down and rebuild it, and you, a young Meccan, are part of the great work!

**GAMEPLAY**
- Carry materials, hand them to the craftsmen, raise the wall. Just stand on the circles and loading happens by itself.
- Spend your honest "bereket" on bigger loads, extra stonecutters and porters.
- Bring Zamzam water to thirsty craftsmen. Calm the clans when tempers flare.
- 7 chapters, each with a new mechanic and 3 stars to earn.

**TRUE HISTORY**
Every chapter is based on a real event from the classical Sira:
1. The Flood
2. Taking It Down
3. The Stonecutters
4. The Blood Oath and the Black Stone
5. The Ship and the carpenter Baqum
6. Rising Walls
7. Roof and Door

Each chapter opens with a plain-language story card and ends with a "Did you know?" fact. Cutscenes are short, comic-style and easy to follow.

**WITH RESPECT**
The Prophet (pbuh) and his Companions are never depicted; events involving them are only narrated. History follows Ibn Hisham's Sira and the TDV Encyclopedia of Islam.

No ads. No account. Plays offline. Turkish and English.
